## 📌 About Sangam

**Sangam** is an AI-powered student career and professional development platform designed initially for the Nepalese student ecosystem.

The platform brings together:

- Students
- Organizations
- Companies
- Colleges and universities
- Alumni
- Mentors
- Communities
- Projects
- Hackathons
- Jobs
- Internships
- Career opportunities
- Professional portfolios
- AI-powered career assistance

Instead of forcing students to use many different platforms for opportunities, networking, projects, resumes, mentorship, and career planning, Sangam aims to provide these experiences through one connected ecosystem.
